📓 Lab Journal — Experiment 8

Experiment 8: Effect of Various Drugs on Rabbit's Eye

Aim: To study the effect of autonomic drugs and local anaesthetics on the pupil diameter, light reflex, and corneal reflex of the rabbit's eye.

Animal: Albino Rabbit (unpigmented iris provides contrast for measurement)

Control: Left Eye — Normal Saline (0.9%)

Test: Right Eye — Drug under investigation

Observation Table

Drug Eye Class / Mechanism Baseline Pupil Post-Drug Pupil Light Reflex Corneal Reflex

Key Conclusions

  • Atropine vs Ephedrine: Both produce mydriasis but only Atropine abolishes the light reflex (M₃ block).
  • Physostigmine: Causes miosis via AChE inhibition (↑ACh at M₃).
  • Lignocaine: Only drug to abolish corneal reflex (Na⁺ channel block) without pupil change.
